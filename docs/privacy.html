Privacy Policy

Puff · last updated: November 14, 2026

Overview

Puff is a single-player game that plays fully offline. It does not collect personal information, and there is no Puff account. To learn where new players get stuck and how the game is played, Puff sends anonymous gameplay statistics, described below, which you can turn off at any time. Apart from those, the only things that can leave your device are your scores and achievements, sent to Apple Game Center if you are signed in to it.

Anonymous gameplay statistics

What is sent. Short records of moments in the game: the app was opened or went to the background, which screen was shown (main menu, game, pause, game over, settings) and for how many seconds, how many milliseconds the main menu took to load, a run started or ended (free or weekly run, environment, blast modifier and colour palette, how the run ended, seconds played, the highest heat level reached, the score and the best combo), and the first time in a session you used a part of the game such as a picker, the leaderboard, sharing or a setting. Each record also carries the app version, the platform (iOS or Android), the app language, and two coarse bands: roughly how many times Puff has been played on this device (1, 2, 3–5, 6–10, 11+) and roughly how many days ago measuring began (0, 1, 2–6, 7–29, 30+). The full list is published in the game's source repository (docs/telemetry.md).

Why. To see what new players do after the first launch, how long runs and sessions last, when players stop and which parts of the game nobody finds, so the game can be improved.

No identifiers. The statistics contain no name, no email, no text of any kind, no account, no device or advertising identifier and no device model. Each play session gets a random number that exists only while the app runs, so two sessions cannot be linked to each other or to you. The statistics are not used for advertising or tracking and are not sold.

Who receives them, and where. The statistics are received and stored by the developer on its own server in the EU (Czech Republic). Nobody else receives them. Sending them, like any internet connection, shows the server your IP address; it is not stored or logged. The server keeps only the country the connection comes from (approximate location: no city, region or coordinates) with each record.

How long. Statistics are kept for 12 months and then deleted. Only aggregate counts with no session number are kept longer.

Offline. The game works fully offline. Without a connection, up to 500 recent records wait on your device and are sent later; older ones are discarded.

How to turn them off. Open Settings from the main menu or the pause screen and switch ANONYMOUS STATS off (the label is translated into your language). This takes effect immediately: records waiting on your device are deleted and nothing more is sent, until you turn it back on.

Data we do not collect

  • Personal identifiers — name, email address, phone number, account.
  • Precise location (only the country, as above).
  • Device or advertising identifiers, or the device model.
  • Crash reports.
  • Contacts, photos, microphone or camera — none of these are requested.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (environment, blast modifier, colour palette, render quality, sound and haptics) and your best score per environment are written to standard platform storage — NSUserDefaults on iOS, SharedPreferences on Android. This data stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app. The statistics keep a few small values there too — the on/off switch, the day measuring began, a play-session counter and the records not sent yet — from which the bands above are worked out on your device.

Network access

Puff is fully playable in airplane mode. When a connection is available it downloads one small settings file from olin.now (which seasonal colour palettes are on, whether the weekly challenge runs). The request carries nothing about you or your play; like any web request, it shows the server your IP address.

On iPhone and iPad, if you are signed in to Game Center, the score of each finished run and any achievement you unlock are sent to Game Center so they can appear on its leaderboards and achievement list.

When you tap Share after a new best, Puff hands a picture of your score and a store link to your device's share sheet. Where it goes from there is your choice; Puff sends nothing on its own.

Third-party services

Apple Game Center, on iPhone and iPad only. You sign in to it in the Settings app, not in Puff, and Apple handles your Game Center profile and scores under the Apple Privacy Policy. Puff sends it nothing but your scores and achievements and receives no personal information from it. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent.

The anonymous statistics go to the developer's own server, as described above, not to a third party. Puff integrates no third-party analytics, advertising, attribution or tracking SDKs, and no third-party fonts or assets are downloaded at runtime.

Tracking

Puff does not track you across apps or websites, so it never presents the App Tracking Transparency prompt and requests no tracking permission.

Children

Puff is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children and is not offered in the App Store Kids category or Google Play's Families programme. It knowingly collects no personal information from anyone, children included: the anonymous statistics described above contain nothing that identifies a person, and they can be turned off in Settings.
