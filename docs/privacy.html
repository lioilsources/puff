Privacy Policy

Puff · last updated: September 6, 2026

Overview

Puff is a single-player offline game. It does not collect, store or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in and no server behind the app.

Data we do not collect

  • Personal identifiers — name, email address, phone number.
  • Location data of any kind.
  • Device or advertising identifiers.
  • Usage analytics, telemetry or crash reports.
  • Contacts, photos, microphone or camera — none of these are requested.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (environment, blast modifier, colour palette, render quality, sound and haptics) and your best score per environment are written to standard platform storage — NSUserDefaults on iOS, SharedPreferences on Android. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Network access

Puff contains no networking code. It makes no requests to any server, including ours, and is fully playable in airplane mode.

Third-party services

None. Puff integrates no analytics, advertising, attribution or tracking SDKs, and no third-party fonts or assets are downloaded at runtime.

Tracking

Puff does not track you across apps or websites, so it never presents the App Tracking Transparency prompt and requests no tracking permission.

Children

Puff is suitable for all ages and knowingly collects no information from anyone, children included — because it collects nothing at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new date.
