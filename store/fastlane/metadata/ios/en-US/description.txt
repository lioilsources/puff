Puff is a minimalist neon physics game: hold, drag, release.

• Hold to charge a pressure cloud, drag it where it matters, release to blast the shapes drifting toward the core
• Hold too long and it fizzles: when do you let go?
• Four environments (vacuum, air, water, plasma), five modifiers and six colour palettes
• A weekly challenge: the same run for every player
• No ads, no tracking, works offline
